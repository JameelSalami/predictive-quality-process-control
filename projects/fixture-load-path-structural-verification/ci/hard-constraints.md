# Hard Constraints

These conditions are feasibility gates, not weighted preferences.

## Safety

- No solver result or analytical calculation is a certified structural-adequacy, life-safety, or production-release claim.
- A future load test must not require a person to manually restrain the specimen under load.

## Legal / regulatory

- No regulated, flight-safety, pressure-vessel, or life-safety application is in scope.

## Quality

- Analytical and numerical projections bind to the same frozen geometry/material/load revision.
- Idealization loss is explicit.
- Physical validity requires measured or trusted benchmark correlation with uncertainty.
- The current local peak stress is not accepted as converged.

## Worker / human

- Physical measurement setup should avoid repeated manual repositioning and ambiguous readings.
- Discrepancy review must remain explainable rather than force a pass/fail from one contour plot.

## Ecological

- Material mass may be compared, but no embodied-carbon or lifecycle benefit is claimed without a defensible source/model.

## Data / privacy

- Public or independently generated synthetic inputs only.
